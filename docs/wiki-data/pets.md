# Pets

_Transcribed from the Overgeared Hero wiki ([Pets](https://overgearedhero.fandom.com/wiki/Pets)) on 2026-10-15. Wiki data is unverified; see REVIEW.md for known problems._

- Up to **3 pets** equipped. Each active skill costs 1–3 activation points; each hero has **5 points** per run.
- Stars **0–6**. Extra effects unlock at the star counts shown (★ = 1 star).
- Pet levels and ownership effects only raise HP/ATK and are already in Stat Details, so they’re ignored.
- Many skills only affect the shop or merging (economy, [LATER]). Pets that put items in the bag or act in battle matter for the MVP.

## Pets (28)

### Venomous Spider

- **Rarity:** Mythic · **Activation cost:** 2
- **Active skill — Raising Spiderlings:** Immediately summons 12 Venomous Spider Egg Sacks into your backpack.
- **Star effects:**
  - 1★: Additionally summons 1 Hefty Egg Sack that is permanently maintained.
  - 2★: Base Cooldown decreases by 2s.
  - 4★: Summons 4 additional Egg Sacks.
  - 5★: Summoned Spiders inflict x2 Poison.

### Geezer

- **Rarity:** Mythic · **Activation cost:** 2
- **Active skill — Geezer's Dream:** Immediately opens a Special Shop. You can purchase 1 of the 3 merged items displayed at a discounted price.
- **Star effects:**
  - 1★: Opens a Special Shop where 1 item is free.
  - 2★: Opens a Special Shop that displays 1 more item.
  - 4★: Let's you reroll the Special Shop for free up to 3 times.
  - 5★: Activation Cost is decreased by 1.

- **Rarity:** Mythic · **Activation cost:** 2
- **Active skill — Mimic!!!:** Immediately merge all mergeable items.
- **Star effects:**

  - 2★: Activated in a round has a 50% chance to be reusable during that round.
  - 4★: Reuse Rate increases by 50%.
  - 5★: Grants 1 Coin each time an item is merged by it.
