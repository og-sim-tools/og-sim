- Classes: Warrior, Ranger, Wizard. Stars **0–9**.
- **Supply Ability** (starting-equipment capacity) = base (Rare 5, Epic 8, Legendary 10) + 1 per star.
- **Supplies** unlock at the stars shown. Once unlocked, a supply can be brought by any hero of the same class.
- **Skill links:** this hero’s skills become linkable by same-class heroes at 4★ and 8★, in the order shown. The hero’s own link slots also open at 4★ and 8★.
- **Hero-exclusive items:** usable as supplies by any same-class hero; can’t be merged; only in shops for their own hero.
- Ownership effects and skins are ignored. 23 heroes here; the wiki’s “26” includes 2 skins and 1 unknown.
