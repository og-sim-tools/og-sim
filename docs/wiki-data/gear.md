**Not on the wiki, so every gear piece below still needs, entered by hand:** shape, item dots, and for weapons the basic attack stats (DMG ratio, cooldown, accuracy, crit rate, hits per attack).

Bonus stats and plain stat lines in "effects by rarity" (e.g. `ATK +25%`) are already included in Stat Details, so the simulator doesn’t apply them again; they are marked _(stat only)_.

## Rarity and tiers

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic. Tiers: Epic +0…+2, Legendary +0…+3, Mythic +0…+4.

**Effects by rarity:** each rarity adds the effect listed for it, and keeps all lower ones. S-rank gear (marked ⭐S) can only be obtained from certain chests.

## Weapons (20)

All weapons share the same base stats: DMG, Cooldown, Accuracy, and Crit Rate. When a weapon attacks multiple times, the listed DMG multiplier applies to each hit. They all increase ATK upon being equipped and each has a unique bonus stat.
